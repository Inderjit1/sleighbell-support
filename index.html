Sleighbell Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Sleighbell has no accounts and no ads. Names you type for Nice List certificates and the advent doors you open stay on your device. They are never sent to us.

Anonymous usage data

We use TelemetryDeck to count anonymous events, such as which screen was opened or whether a door was opened. These events contain no names, no free text and no advertising identifiers, are not linked to your identity, and are not used to track you.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Sharing

A certificate image leaves your device only when you choose to share it.
